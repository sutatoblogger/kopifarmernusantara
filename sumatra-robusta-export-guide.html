The 2026 Guide to Importing Premium Sumatra Robusta Greenbeans

International commodity trading leaves zero room for administrative or logistical errors. Many first-time importers face severe delays at the port of destination simply because their supplier failed to maintain proper moisture levels or provided inadequate documentation. When dealing with wholesale Sumatra robusta coffee beans, precision is non-negotiable.

1. Mastering Quality Control & Grading

Global industrial roasteries demand absolute consistency. If your Letter of Intent (LOI) specifies robusta greenbeans grade 1, the defect value must strictly remain below 11. This requires advanced sorting facilities that calibrate the beans specifically to Screen Size 17-19 before they are packed into export-standard 60kg jute bags. Ignoring this standard often leads to rejected shipments upon arrival.

2. Navigating Export Documentation

Shipping under FOB Tanjung Priok (Jakarta) or CIF terms requires the exporter to handle complex maritime bureaucracy. For shipments heading to our primary markets in the UAE, Egypt, Fujian (China), France, and Canada, the presence of a valid Phytosanitary Certificate and a Certificate of Origin (COO) is mandatory. Without these clean shipping documents, maritime insurance will not cover the transnational voyage.

3. The Importance of Third-Party Inspections

Never rely solely on a supplier's word. Credible exporters always accommodate third-party maritime surveyors. Conducting a thorough SGS or Sucofindo inspection during container stuffing guarantees that the exact tonnage and quality specifications outlined in your contract are fulfilled before the vessel departs.
