Sourcing Premium Coconut Shell Charcoal for the Middle East Shisha Market

The global hookah and shisha industry is unforgiving when it comes to quality. For premium lounges across the UAE, Egypt, and Europe, the charcoal used is just as important as the flavor of the tobacco itself. Importers constantly face the challenge of finding suppliers who do not mix wood or sawdust into their briquettes.

The "Fixed Carbon" Requirement

The secret to a long-lasting, odorless, and smokeless burn lies in the Fixed Carbon percentage. A true premium briquette made from 100% mature Indonesian coconut shells must boast a fixed carbon rate exceeding 75%. This ensures the cube burns evenly for up to 2.5 hours without altering the taste of the shisha.

Understanding Ash Content & Color

Low-tier charcoal leaves behind a heavy, dark brown ash that constantly drops, requiring staff to clean it frequently. Top-tier coconut shell charcoal briquettes leave a very thin layer of white or light grey ash, strictly kept under the 2.5% threshold.
